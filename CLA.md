## How to accept

On your first pull request, the CLA bot posts a comment. Reply with exactly:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The bot records your GitHub account, the date and the pull request, and marks the check as passed
on that and future pull requests. Questions: legal@orkstage.io.
