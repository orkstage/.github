# OrkStage trademark policy

Version 1.0

"OrkStage" and the OrkStage logos (the "Marks") belong to Paulo Costa, who publishes OrkStage. The
code is open source; the name and logos are not. This policy explains how you can use them, so that
people can always tell what comes from the OrkStage project and what does not.

## Uses that are always fine

- Saying truthfully that your product works with, integrates with, or was built on OrkStage, for
  example "Deploy templates to OrkStage" or "A Terraform provider for OrkStage".
- Writing about OrkStage: articles, talks, tutorials, courses, reviews and comparisons.
- Naming community groups, meetups and events about OrkStage, as long as they are free or not
  for profit and say that they are not run by the project, for example "OrkStage Users São Paulo".
- Distributing OrkStage without changes, under its original name, from the project's releases or
  source code.
- Using the logo next to such a mention, without changing its colors, proportions or shapes (the
  files are in `orkstage-site/public/brand/`).

## Uses that need permission

Ask at legal@orkstage.io first for:

- Selling or hosting a product or service whose name includes "OrkStage", for example a managed
  "OrkStage Cloud" from someone else.
- Merchandise with the Marks.
- Paid training or certification that uses the Marks in its name.

## Uses that are not allowed

- Naming a modified version, a fork or a different product "OrkStage" or something confusingly
  similar ("Orkstage Pro", "OrkStager", "OrkStage Community Edition" for your fork). A fork must use
  another name and logo; it may say that it is "based on OrkStage".
- Domain names, package names, social media accounts or app names that suggest an official
  connection (`orkstage-cloud.com`, `@orkstage_official`).
- Using the Marks in a way that suggests the project endorses, certifies or supports you when it
  does not, or in a misleading or disparaging way.
- Changing the logos or combining them with your own marks.

## Notes

- This policy does not limit what the code licenses allow you to do with the code itself. It only
  covers the name and the logos.
- When in doubt, write "based on OrkStage" or "for OrkStage" rather than using the name as your
  product's name, and ask: legal@orkstage.io.
- This policy may be updated; the version in this repository applies.
